=== 1 | 3d | 2 | sub
@topic Equation of a line
Find the vector equation of a line passing trough a point with position vector $2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + \hat{k}$ and parallel to the line joining the points $-\hat{i} + 4\hat{j} + \hat{k}$ and $\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$.
--- ans
Let the points be $A(2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + \hat{k})$, $B(-\hat{i} + 4\hat{j} + \hat{k})$ and $C(\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + 2\hat{k})$.
$$\overrightarrow{BC} = (\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}) - (-\hat{i} + 4\hat{j} + \hat{k}) = 2\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + \hat{k}$$
The line through $\vec{a}$ parallel to $\vec{b}$ is $\vec{r} = \vec{a} + \lambda\vec{b}$:
$$\vec{r} = (2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + \hat{k}) + \lambda(2\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + \hat{k})$$

=== 9a | vec | 3 | sub
@topic Vector product and area
Let $\vec{a} = \hat{i} + \hat{j}$, $\vec{b} = \hat{i} - \hat{j}$ and $\vec{c} = \hat{i} + \hat{j} + \hat{k}$. If $\hat{n}$ is a unit vector such that $\vec{a} \cdot \hat{n} = 0$ and $\vec{b} \cdot \hat{n} = 0$, then find $|\vec{c} \cdot \hat{n}|$.
--- ans
$\hat{n}$ is perpendicular to both $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$, so $\hat{n} = \pm\dfrac{\vec{a} \times \vec{b}}{|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}|}$.
$$\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & -1 & 0 \end{vmatrix} = 0\hat{i} - 0\hat{j} + (-1 - 1)\hat{k} = -2\hat{k}$$
$|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}| = 2 \Rightarrow \hat{n} = \pm\hat{k}$.
$$|\vec{c} \cdot \hat{n}| = \left|(\hat{i} + \hat{j} + \hat{k}) \cdot (\pm\hat{k})\right| = |\pm 1| = 1$$

=== 9b | vec | 3 | sub
@topic Vector product and area
If $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$ are unit vectors inclined at an angle $30^\circ$ to each other, then find the area of the parallelogram with $(\vec{a} + 3\vec{b})$ and $(3\vec{a} + \vec{b})$ as adjacent sides.
--- ans
Area of a parallelogram with adjacent sides $\vec{p}, \vec{q}$ is $|\vec{p} \times \vec{q}|$.
$$(\vec{a} + 3\vec{b}) \times (3\vec{a} + \vec{b}) = 3\,\vec{a} \times \vec{a} + \vec{a} \times \vec{b} + 9\,\vec{b} \times \vec{a} + 3\,\vec{b} \times \vec{b}$$
$$= \vec{0} + \vec{a} \times \vec{b} - 9\,\vec{a} \times \vec{b} + \vec{0} = -8\,(\vec{a} \times \vec{b})$$
$$\text{Area} = 8|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}| = 8|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\sin 30^\circ = 8\cdot 1\cdot 1\cdot\frac{1}{2} = 4\ \text{sq. units}$$

=== 10 | int | 3 | sub
@topic Properties of definite integrals
Evaluate: $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{1}{1 + (\tan x)^{2/3}}\,dx$
--- ans
$$I = \int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{1}{1 + (\tan x)^{2/3}}\,dx \quad\cdots(1)$$
Using $\displaystyle\int_0^a f(x)\,dx = \int_0^a f(a - x)\,dx$:
$$I = \int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{1}{1 + (\cot x)^{2/3}}\,dx = \int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{(\tan x)^{2/3}}{(\tan x)^{2/3} + 1}\,dx \quad\cdots(2)$$
Adding (1) and (2):
$$2I = \int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{1 + (\tan x)^{2/3}}{1 + (\tan x)^{2/3}}\,dx = \int_0^{\pi/2} 1\,dx = \frac{\pi}{2}$$
$$\therefore\ I = \frac{\pi}{4}$$

=== 13 | prob | 4 | sub
@topic Total probability and Bayes' theorem
In a factory, machine A produces 30% of total output, machine B produces 25% and the machine C produces the remaining output. The defective items produced by machines A, B and C are 1%, 1.2%, 2% respectively. An item is picked at random from a day's output and found to be defective. Find the probability that it was produced by machine B?
--- ans
Let $E_1, E_2, E_3$: the item is produced by machine A, B, C respectively; $D$: the item is defective.
$$P(E_1) = 0.30,\quad P(E_2) = 0.25,\quad P(E_3) = 1 - 0.55 = 0.45$$
$$P(D|E_1) = 0.01,\quad P(D|E_2) = 0.012,\quad P(D|E_3) = 0.02$$
By Bayes' theorem:
$$P(E_2|D) = \frac{P(E_2)P(D|E_2)}{P(E_1)P(D|E_1) + P(E_2)P(D|E_2) + P(E_3)P(D|E_3)}$$
$$= \frac{0.25\times 0.012}{0.30\times 0.01 + 0.25\times 0.012 + 0.45\times 0.02} = \frac{0.003}{0.003 + 0.003 + 0.009} = \frac{0.003}{0.015} = \frac{1}{5}$$
Required probability $= \dfrac{1}{5}$.
