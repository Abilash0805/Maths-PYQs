@topic Rate of change
If the distance travelled by a particle in t seconds is given by $S = 72t + 3t^2 - t^3$, then time taken by the particle to come to rest is :
(A) $4$ seconds
(B) $6$ seconds
(C) $3$ seconds
(D) $0$ seconds
--- ans B
Velocity $v = \dfrac{dS}{dt} = 72 + 6t - 3t^2$.
The particle comes to rest when $v = 0$: $3t^2 - 6t - 72 = 0 \Rightarrow t^2 - 2t - 24 = 0 \Rightarrow (t - 6)(t + 4) = 0$.
Since $t > 0$, $t = 6$ seconds.

@topic Order and degree
The sum of the order and the degree of the differential equation
$$4\left[\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}\right]^2 + 3\left[1 + \left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2 - y\right] = 0 \text{ is :}$$
(A) $2$
(B) $3$
(C) not defined
(D) $4$
--- ans D
The highest order derivative is $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}$, so order $= 2$. The equation is a polynomial in the derivatives and the highest order derivative occurs with power 2, so degree $= 2$.
Sum $= 2 + 2 = 4$.

@topic Multiplication theorem and independent events
The probability that it will rain tomorrow in cities A, B and C is 60%, 70% and 80% respectively. The probability that it will rain tomorrow in at least one of the cities is :
(A) $\dfrac{3}{250}$
(B) $\dfrac{244}{250}$
(C) $1$
(D) $\dfrac{9}{10}$
--- ans B
Treating rain in the three cities as independent events, P(no rain in A, B, C) $= 0.4 \times 0.3 \times 0.2 = 0.024$.
$$P(\text{rain in at least one city}) = 1 - 0.024 = 0.976 = \frac{244}{250}$$

=== 23 | aod | 2 | sub
@topic Increasing and decreasing functions
Find the interval(s) in which the function $f(x) = \dfrac{x}{\log x}$, where $x \in (0, 1) \cup (1, \infty)$, is increasing.
--- ans
$$f'(x) = \frac{\log x\cdot 1 - x\cdot\frac{1}{x}}{(\log x)^2} = \frac{\log x - 1}{(\log x)^2}$$
$f'(x) = 0 \Rightarrow \log x = 1 \Rightarrow x = e$.
Since $(\log x)^2 > 0$ for $x \ne 1$, the sign of $f'(x)$ is the sign of $\log x - 1$:
for $x > e$, $\log x > 1 \Rightarrow f'(x) > 0$; for $0 < x < 1$ and $1 < x < e$, $\log x < 1 \Rightarrow f'(x) < 0$.
$\therefore f$ is **increasing on $(e, \infty)$** (or $[e, \infty)$).

@topic Position vectors and section formula
If $(2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + \hat{k})$ and $(2\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k})$ represent the sides $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$ respectively of $\triangle ABC$, find the vector representing the median through A.
--- ans
Let D be the mid-point of BC; the median through A is $\overrightarrow{AD}$.
Taking A as origin, the position vectors of B and C are $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$, so by the mid-point formula
$$\overrightarrow{AD} = \frac{\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC}}{2} = \frac{(2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + \hat{k}) + (2\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k})}{2} = \frac{4\hat{i} + 4\hat{j}}{2}$$
$$\boxed{\overrightarrow{AD} = 2\hat{i} + 2\hat{j}}$$

@topic Operations on matrices
If $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$, then compute $A^2 - 4A - 5I$.
--- ans
$$A^2 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 + 4 + 4 & 2 + 2 + 4 & 2 + 4 + 2 \\ 2 + 2 + 4 & 4 + 1 + 4 & 4 + 2 + 2 \\ 2 + 4 + 2 & 4 + 2 + 2 & 4 + 4 + 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 9 & 8 & 8 \\ 8 & 9 & 8 \\ 8 & 8 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$$
$$4A = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 8 & 8 \\ 8 & 4 & 8 \\ 8 & 8 & 4 \end{bmatrix},\qquad 5I = \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 5 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 5 \end{bmatrix}$$
$$A^2 - 4A - 5I = \begin{bmatrix} 9 - 4 - 5 & 8 - 8 - 0 & 8 - 8 - 0 \\ 8 - 8 - 0 & 9 - 4 - 5 & 8 - 8 - 0 \\ 8 - 8 - 0 & 8 - 8 - 0 & 9 - 4 - 5 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$
$\therefore$ **$A^2 - 4A - 5I = O$** (the zero matrix).

@topic Area under a curve
Using integration, find the area of the region bounded by the curve $y = x|x|$, x-axis, $x = -2$ and $x = 2$.
--- ans
$y = x|x| = \begin{cases} -x^2, & x < 0 \\ x^2, & x \ge 0 \end{cases}$
For $-2 \le x < 0$ the curve lies below the x-axis and for $0 \le x \le 2$ above it.
```plot
{"x":[-2.6,2.6],"y":[-4.6,4.6],"fns":[{"f":"x*abs(x)","from":-2.3,"to":2.3,"label":"y = x|x|"}],"areas":[{"upper":"0","lower":"x*abs(x)","from":-2,"to":0},{"upper":"x*abs(x)","lower":"0","from":0,"to":2}],"points":[[-2,-4,"(−2, −4)"],[2,4,"(2, 4)"],[0,0,"O"]]}
```
$$\text{Area} = \int_{-2}^{0}|-x^2|\,dx + \int_0^2 x^2\,dx = \int_{-2}^{0}x^2\,dx + \int_0^2 x^2\,dx$$
$$= \left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_{-2}^{0} + \left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^2 = \frac{8}{3} + \frac{8}{3} = \frac{16}{3}$$
**Required area $= \dfrac{16}{3}$ sq. units.**

@topic Intersection and collinearity
Find the vector and cartesian equations of the line passing through the point of intersection of the lines $\vec{r} = (\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k}) + \lambda(3\hat{i} - \hat{j})$ and $\vec{r} = (4\hat{i} - \hat{k}) + \mu(2\hat{i} + 3\hat{k})$ and parallel to the line $\dfrac{x - 1}{-2} = \dfrac{7 - y}{-3} = z$.
--- ans
General points: first line $(1 + 3\lambda,\ 1 - \lambda,\ -1)$; second line $(4 + 2\mu,\ 0,\ -1 + 3\mu)$.
At the point of intersection:
$$y:\ 1 - \lambda = 0 \Rightarrow \lambda = 1,\qquad z:\ -1 = -1 + 3\mu \Rightarrow \mu = 0$$
Check $x$: $1 + 3(1) = 4 = 4 + 2(0)$ ✓. So the lines intersect at $(4, 0, -1)$.
The given line in standard form is $\dfrac{x - 1}{-2} = \dfrac{y - 7}{3} = \dfrac{z}{1}$, so its direction ratios are $-2, 3, 1$.
Required line through $(4, 0, -1)$ parallel to $-2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + \hat{k}$:
**Vector form:** $\vec{r} = (4\hat{i} - \hat{k}) + t(-2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + \hat{k})$
**Cartesian form:** $\dfrac{x - 4}{-2} = \dfrac{y}{3} = \dfrac{z + 1}{1}$
