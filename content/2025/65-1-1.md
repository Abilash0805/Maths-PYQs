$\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1} \frac{|x|}{x}\,dx,\ x \ne 0$ is equal to:
(A) $-1$
(B) $0$
(C) $1$

Differentiate $2^{\cos^2 x}$ w.r.t. $\cos^2 x$.
--- ans
Let $u = \cos^2 x$ and $y = 2^{\cos^2 x} = 2^u$.

Find : $\displaystyle\int \frac{x + \sin x}{1 + \cos x}\,dx$
--- ans
