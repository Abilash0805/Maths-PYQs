@topic Vector product and area
The unit vector perpendicular to the vectors $\hat{i} - \hat{j}$ and $\hat{i} + \hat{j}$ is
(A) $\hat{k}$
(B) $-\hat{k} + \hat{j}$
(C) $\dfrac{\hat{i} - \hat{j}}{\sqrt{2}}$
(D) $\dfrac{\hat{i} + \hat{j}}{\sqrt{2}}$
--- ans A
$$(\hat{i} - \hat{j}) \times (\hat{i} + \hat{j}) = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 1 & -1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \end{vmatrix} = 2\hat{k}$$
Unit vector $= \dfrac{2\hat{k}}{2} = \hat{k}$ (or $-\hat{k}$). (B) is not a unit vector; (C), (D) are not perpendicular to both.

@topic Integration by parts
If $\displaystyle\int_0^1 \frac{e^x}{1 + x}\,dx = \alpha$, then $\displaystyle\int_0^1 \frac{e^x}{(1 + x)^2}\,dx$ is equal to
(A) $\alpha - 1 + \dfrac{e}{2}$
(B) $\alpha + 1 - \dfrac{e}{2}$
(C) $\alpha - 1 - \dfrac{e}{2}$
(D) $\alpha + 1 + \dfrac{e}{2}$
--- ans B
Integrating by parts with $\frac{1}{1 + x}$ as the first function:
$$\alpha = \int_0^1 \frac{e^x}{1 + x}\,dx = \left[\frac{e^x}{1 + x}\right]_0^1 + \int_0^1 \frac{e^x}{(1 + x)^2}\,dx = \frac{e}{2} - 1 + \int_0^1 \frac{e^x}{(1 + x)^2}\,dx$$
$\therefore \displaystyle\int_0^1 \frac{e^x}{(1 + x)^2}\,dx = \alpha + 1 - \frac{e}{2}$. (The publisher's key (C) is a sign slip.)

@topic Operations on matrices
If $\begin{bmatrix} x + y & 3y \\ 3x & x + 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 9 & 4x + y \\ x + 6 & y \end{bmatrix}$, then $(x - y) = ?$
(A) $-7$
(B) $-3$
(C) $3$
(D) $7$
--- ans B
Equating entries: $3x = x + 6 \Rightarrow x = 3$; $3y = 4x + y \Rightarrow y = 2x = 6$. (Check: $x + y = 9$ ✓, $x + 3 = 6 = y$ ✓.)
$x - y = 3 - 6 = -3$.

=== 7 | prob | 1 | mcq
@topic Conditional probability
Let $M$ and $N$ be two events such that $P(M) = 0.6$, $P(N) = 0.2$ and $P(M \cap N) = 0.5$, then $P(M'/N')$ is
(A) $\dfrac{7}{8}$
(B) $\dfrac{2}{5}$
(C) $\dfrac{1}{2}$
(D) $\dfrac{2}{3}$
--- ans A
$P(M \cup N) = 0.6 + 0.2 - 0.5 = 0.3$.
$$P(M' \mid N') = \frac{P(M' \cap N')}{P(N')} = \frac{1 - P(M \cup N)}{1 - P(N)} = \frac{0.7}{0.8} = \frac{7}{8}$$
(Strictly, the data are inconsistent, since $P(M \cap N)$ cannot exceed $P(N) = 0.2$; the intended answer, by the formula, is $\frac{7}{8}$.)

=== 11 | det | 1 | mcq
@topic Systems of linear equations
A system of linear equations is represented as $AX = B$, where $A$ is coefficient matrix, $X$ is variable matrix and $B$ is the constant matrix. Then the system of equations is
(A) Consistent, if $|A| \ne 0$, solution is given by $X = BA^{-1}$.
(B) Inconsistent if $|A| = 0$ and $(\operatorname{adj}A)B = 0$
(C) Inconsistent if $|A| \ne 0$
(D) May or may not be consistent if $|A| = 0$ and $(\operatorname{adj}A)B = 0$
--- ans D
If $|A| \ne 0$ the system is consistent with the unique solution $X = A^{-1}B$ (not $BA^{-1}$), so (A) and (C) are false.
If $|A| = 0$ and $(\operatorname{adj}A)B = O$, the system may have infinitely many solutions or no solution — it may or may not be consistent. So (B) is false and (D) is true.

=== 22 | aod | 2 | sub
@topic Increasing and decreasing functions
Find the values of '$a$' for which $f(x) = \sqrt{3}\sin x - \cos x - 2ax + b$ is decreasing on $\mathbb{R}$.
--- ans
$$f'(x) = \sqrt{3}\cos x + \sin x - 2a = 2\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos x + \frac{1}{2}\sin x\right) - 2a = 2\sin\left(x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) - 2a$$
$f$ is decreasing on $\mathbb{R}$ if $f'(x) \le 0$ for all $x$, i.e. $\sin\left(x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) \le a$ for all $x$.
Since the maximum value of $\sin\left(x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right)$ is 1, this requires $a \ge 1$.
**Hence $a \ge 1$, i.e. $a \in [1, \infty)$.**

@topic Partial fractions
Find : $\displaystyle\int \frac{2x - 1}{(x - 1)(x + 2)(x - 3)}\,dx$
--- ans
Let $\dfrac{2x - 1}{(x - 1)(x + 2)(x - 3)} = \dfrac{A}{x - 1} + \dfrac{B}{x + 2} + \dfrac{C}{x - 3}$, so $2x - 1 = A(x + 2)(x - 3) + B(x - 1)(x - 3) + C(x - 1)(x + 2)$.
$x = 1$: $1 = -6A \Rightarrow A = -\dfrac{1}{6}$. $\quad x = -2$: $-5 = 15B \Rightarrow B = -\dfrac{1}{3}$. $\quad x = 3$: $5 = 10C \Rightarrow C = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
$$\int \frac{2x - 1}{(x - 1)(x + 2)(x - 3)}\,dx = \boxed{-\frac{1}{6}\log|x - 1| - \frac{1}{3}\log|x + 2| + \frac{1}{2}\log|x - 3| + C}$$

@topic Properties of definite integrals
Evaluate : $\displaystyle\int_0^5 \left(|x - 1| + |x - 2| + |x - 5|\right)dx$
--- ans
$$\int_0^5 |x - 1|\,dx = \int_0^1(1 - x)\,dx + \int_1^5(x - 1)\,dx = \frac{1}{2} + 8 = \frac{17}{2}$$
$$\int_0^5 |x - 2|\,dx = \int_0^2(2 - x)\,dx + \int_2^5(x - 2)\,dx = 2 + \frac{9}{2} = \frac{13}{2}$$
$$\int_0^5 |x - 5|\,dx = \int_0^5(5 - x)\,dx = \left[5x - \frac{x^2}{2}\right]_0^5 = \frac{25}{2}$$
$$\therefore\ I = \frac{17}{2} + \frac{13}{2} + \frac{25}{2} = \boxed{\frac{55}{2}}$$

=== 28 | aod | 3 | sub
@topic Rate of change
A spherical medicine ball when dropped in water dissolves in such a way that the rate of decrease of volume at any instant is proportional to its surface area. Calculate that rate of decrease of its radius.
--- ans
Let the radius be $r$, volume $V = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$, surface area $S = 4\pi r^2$.
Given $\dfrac{dV}{dt} = -kS = -4k\pi r^2$, where $k > 0$ is the constant of proportionality.
$$\frac{dV}{dt} = 4\pi r^2\frac{dr}{dt} \Rightarrow 4\pi r^2\frac{dr}{dt} = -4k\pi r^2 \Rightarrow \frac{dr}{dt} = -k$$
**The radius decreases at a constant rate $k$** (the same constant of proportionality), i.e. $-\dfrac{dr}{dt} = k$.

=== 33 | int | 5 | sub
@topic Integration by parts
Find: $\displaystyle\int \sin^{-1}\sqrt{\frac{x}{a + x}}\,dx$.
--- ans
Put $x = a\tan^2\theta$, $dx = 2a\tan\theta\sec^2\theta\,d\theta$. Then $\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{a + x}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{a\tan^2\theta}{a\sec^2\theta}} = \sin\theta$, so $\sin^{-1}\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{a + x}} = \theta$.
$$I = \int \theta\cdot 2a\tan\theta\sec^2\theta\,d\theta$$
By parts, with $\theta$ as first function and $\int 2\tan\theta\sec^2\theta\,d\theta = \tan^2\theta$:
$$I = a\left[\theta\tan^2\theta - \int\tan^2\theta\,d\theta\right] = a\left[\theta\tan^2\theta - \int(\sec^2\theta - 1)\,d\theta\right] = a\left[\theta\tan^2\theta - \tan\theta + \theta\right] + C$$
$$= a\theta(1 + \tan^2\theta) - a\tan\theta + C$$
Back-substituting $\tan\theta = \sqrt{\dfrac{x}{a}}$, $\theta = \tan^{-1}\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{a}}$, $a(1 + \tan^2\theta) = a + x$:
$$\boxed{I = (a + x)\tan^{-1}\sqrt{\frac{x}{a}} - \sqrt{ax} + C}$$
