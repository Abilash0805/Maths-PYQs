@topic Homogeneous differential equation
Find the general solution of the differential equation:
$(xy - x^2)\,dy = y^2\,dx$.
--- ans

$$v + x\frac{dv}{dx} = \frac{v^2}{v - 1} \Rightarrow x\frac{dv}{dx} = \frac{v^2 - v^2 + v}{v - 1} = \frac{v}{v - 1}$$
