@topic Rate of change of quantities
The radius $r$ of a right circular cylinder is decreasing at the rate of $3$ cm/min. and its height $h$ is increasing at the rate of $2$ cm/min. When $r = 7$ cm and $h = 2$ cm, find the rate of change of the volume of cylinder. [Use $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$]

@topic Increasing and decreasing functions
Show that the function $f$ given by $f(x) = \tan^{-1}(\sin x + \cos x)$ is decreasing for all $x \in \left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$.
--- ans
$$f'(x) = \frac{1}{1 + (\sin x + \cos x)^2}\cdot(\cos x - \sin x)$$
The denominator $1 + (\sin x + \cos x)^2 > 0$ for all $x$.
For $x \in \left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, $\sin x > \cos x$, so $\cos x - \sin x < 0$.
$\therefore f'(x) < 0$ for all $x \in \left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, hence $f$ is **decreasing** in $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$.

@topic Matrix equation – multiplication of matrices
Find matrix X so that $X\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -7 & -8 & -9 \\ 2 & 4 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$.
--- ans
The product of X with a $2 \times 3$ matrix is a $2 \times 3$ matrix, so X is of order $2 \times 2$. Let $X = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$.
$$\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} a + 4b & 2a + 5b & 3a + 6b \\ c + 4d & 2c + 5d & 3c + 6d \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -7 & -8 & -9 \\ 2 & 4 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$$
Equating corresponding elements:
$a + 4b = -7,\quad 2a + 5b = -8,\quad 3a + 6b = -9$
From the first two: $2(-7 - 4b) + 5b = -8 \Rightarrow -3b = 6 \Rightarrow b = -2,\ a = 1$. (Check: $3 - 12 = -9$ ✓)
$c + 4d = 2,\quad 2c + 5d = 4,\quad 3c + 6d = 6$
From the first two: $2(2 - 4d) + 5d = 4 \Rightarrow -3d = 0 \Rightarrow d = 0,\ c = 2$. (Check: $6 + 0 = 6$ ✓)
$$\therefore\ X = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

@topic Logarithmic / implicit differentiation
If $xy = e^{(x - y)}$, then show that $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{y(x - 1)}{x(y + 1)}$.
--- ans
Taking logarithm on both sides:
$$\log x + \log y = x - y$$
Differentiating w.r.t. $x$:
$$\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y}\frac{dy}{dx} = 1 - \frac{dy}{dx}$$
$$\Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx}\left(\frac{1}{y} + 1\right) = 1 - \frac{1}{x} \Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx}\cdot\frac{1 + y}{y} = \frac{x - 1}{x}$$
$$\therefore\ \frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{y(x - 1)}{x(y + 1)}$$
Hence proved.

Find the particular solution of the differential equation $2y\,e^{x/y}\,dx + \left(y - 2x\,e^{x/y}\right)dy = 0$, given that $x = 0$ when $y = 1$.

@topic Bayes' theorem
In a shop X, 30 tins of pure ghee and 40 tins of adulterated ghee which look alike, are kept for sale while in shop Y, similar 50 tins of pure ghee and 60 tins of adulterated ghee are there. One tin of ghee is purchased from one of the randomly selected shops and is found to be adulterated. Find the probability that it is purchased from shop Y. What measures should be taken to stop adulteration?
--- ans
Let $E_1$: shop X is selected, $E_2$: shop Y is selected, $A$: the tin purchased is adulterated.
$$P(E_1) = P(E_2) = \frac{1}{2}$$
$$P(A|E_1) = \frac{40}{70} = \frac{4}{7},\qquad P(A|E_2) = \frac{60}{110} = \frac{6}{11}$$
By Bayes' theorem:
$$P(E_2|A) = \frac{P(E_2)P(A|E_2)}{P(E_1)P(A|E_1) + P(E_2)P(A|E_2)} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{6}{11}}{\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{4}{7} + \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{6}{11}} = \frac{\frac{6}{11}}{\frac{44 + 42}{77}} = \frac{6}{11}\cdot\frac{77}{86} = \frac{21}{43}$$
**Measures to stop adulteration:** regular inspection and sampling of food items by food-safety authorities, strict legal action and heavy penalties against offenders, and creating consumer awareness (buying certified/sealed products and reporting adulteration).

@topic Maxima and minima – word problem
A wire of length 34 m is to be cut into two pieces. One of the pieces is to be made into a square and the other into a rectangle whose length is twice its breadth. What should be the lengths of the two pieces, so that the combined area of the square and the rectangle is minimum?
--- ans
Let the piece made into the square have length $x$ m; the other piece has length $(34 - x)$ m.
Side of the square $= \dfrac{x}{4}$, so its area $= \dfrac{x^2}{16}$.
Let the breadth of the rectangle be $b$; then its length is $2b$ and $2(2b + b) = 34 - x \Rightarrow b = \dfrac{34 - x}{6}$.
Area of rectangle $= 2b \cdot b = 2b^2 = \dfrac{(34 - x)^2}{18}$.
$$A(x) = \frac{x^2}{16} + \frac{(34 - x)^2}{18},\quad 0 < x < 34$$
$$A'(x) = \frac{x}{8} - \frac{34 - x}{9}$$
$A'(x) = 0 \Rightarrow 9x = 8(34 - x) \Rightarrow 17x = 272 \Rightarrow x = 16$.
$$A''(x) = \frac{1}{8} + \frac{1}{9} > 0$$
So $A$ is minimum at $x = 16$.
$\therefore$ The lengths of the two pieces are **16 m** (for the square) and **18 m** (for the rectangle).

@topic Area of a triangle using integration
Using the method of integration, find the area of the triangle ABC, coordinates of whose vertices are $A(1, 2)$, $B(2, 0)$ and $C(4, 3)$.
--- ans
Equations of the sides:
AB: $y = 4 - 2x$, $\quad$ BC: $y = \dfrac{3x}{2} - 3$, $\quad$ AC: $y = \dfrac{x + 5}{3}$.

```plot
{"x":[-0.5,5],"y":[-0.5,4],"lines":[{"a":2,"b":1,"c":4,"label":"AB: y = 4 − 2x"},{"a":3,"b":-2,"c":6,"label":"BC: 3x − 2y = 6"},{"a":1,"b":-3,"c":-5,"label":"AC: x − 3y = −5"}],"polygons":[{"pts":[[1,2],[2,0],[4,3]]}],"points":[{"x":1,"y":2,"label":"A(1, 2)"},{"x":2,"y":0,"label":"B(2, 0)"},{"x":4,"y":3,"label":"C(4, 3)"}]}
```

Area of $\triangle ABC$ = (area under AC from $x = 1$ to $4$) $-$ (area under AB from $1$ to $2$) $-$ (area under BC from $2$ to $4$):
$$\text{Area} = \int_1^4 \frac{x + 5}{3}\,dx - \int_1^2 (4 - 2x)\,dx - \int_2^4 \left(\frac{3x}{2} - 3\right)dx$$
$$= \frac{1}{3}\left[\frac{x^2}{2} + 5x\right]_1^4 - \Big[4x - x^2\Big]_1^2 - \left[\frac{3x^2}{4} - 3x\right]_2^4$$
$$= \frac{1}{3}\left(28 - \frac{11}{2}\right) - (4 - 3) - \big(0 - (-3)\big) = \frac{15}{2} - 1 - 3 = \frac{7}{2}$$
$\therefore$ Area of $\triangle ABC = \dfrac{7}{2}$ sq. units.

@topic Area between a circle and a line
Using integration, find the area of the region $\{(x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \le 1 \le x + y\}$.
--- ans
The region lies inside the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ and on or above the line $x + y = 1$. The line meets the circle at $(1, 0)$ and $(0, 1)$.

```plot
{"x":[-1.5,1.5],"y":[-1.5,1.5],"circles":[{"cx":0,"cy":0,"r":1,"label":"x² + y² = 1"}],"lines":[{"a":1,"b":1,"c":1,"label":"x + y = 1"}],"areas":[{"upper":"sqrt(1-x*x)","lower":"1-x","from":0,"to":1}],"points":[{"x":1,"y":0,"label":"(1, 0)"},{"x":0,"y":1,"label":"(0, 1)"}]}
```

$$\text{Area} = \int_0^1 \left[\sqrt{1 - x^2} - (1 - x)\right]dx$$
$$= \left[\frac{x}{2}\sqrt{1 - x^2} + \frac{1}{2}\sin^{-1}x\right]_0^1 - \left[x - \frac{x^2}{2}\right]_0^1$$
$$= \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{1}{2} = \left(\frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{1}{2}\right)\ \text{sq. units}$$

@topic Linear differential equation – particular solution
Find the particular solution of the differential equation $\dfrac{dy}{dx} - 3y\cot x = \sin 2x$, given that $y = 2$ when $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
--- ans
This is linear: $\dfrac{dy}{dx} + Py = Q$ with $P = -3\cot x$, $Q = \sin 2x$.
$$\text{I.F.} = e^{\int -3\cot x\,dx} = e^{-3\log|\sin x|} = \frac{1}{\sin^3 x} = \operatorname{cosec}^3 x$$
The solution is
$$y\operatorname{cosec}^3 x = \int \sin 2x \cdot \frac{1}{\sin^3 x}\,dx = \int \frac{2\cos x}{\sin^2 x}\,dx = \int 2\operatorname{cosec}x\cot x\,dx = -2\operatorname{cosec}x + C$$
$$\Rightarrow y = -2\sin^2 x + C\sin^3 x$$
Using $y = 2$ when $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$: $2 = -2 + C \Rightarrow C = 4$.
$\therefore$ The particular solution is
$$y = 4\sin^3 x - 2\sin^2 x$$
